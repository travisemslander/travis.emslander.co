While listening to one of my usual podcasts, [they talked about an odd new meme called Italian Brainrot](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zUFGeM50quw).

Apparently it started when someone created a TikTok video featuring an AI-generated character called "Tralalero Tralala," which sparked a trend of additional characters with Italian-sounding names. Very odd, but exactly the type of silly stuff my 13 and 10-year-old sons would normally find hilarious. Unfortunately, when I brought it up to them thinking they'd love it, they told me everyone already knows about it and that I'm old ¯\_(ツ)_/¯
