title: "Teaching a Dog to Vibe Code Games"
date: 2026-03-04T08:15:00-05:00
---

A software engineer decided to [teach his dog to vibe code games](https://www.calebleak.com/posts/dog-game/). Having the same dog breed myself (a cavapoo), I was immediately hooked.

The experiment is delightfully silly: he had his dog tap a keyboard to enter random nonsense strings, then instructed an AI model that the text was a secret cipher representing a brilliant game design that the AI needed to decipher and build. Looking through the resulting games and prompts is both hilarious and surprisingly clever.
