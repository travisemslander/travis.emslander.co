I came across a [collection of best practices for REST APIs](https://github.com/stickfigure/blog/wiki/How-to-%28and-how-not-to%29-design-REST-APIs). While it's one person's opinion, I really enjoy reading through writeups like this because they condense a great deal of practical experience into a concise guide. Each recommendation is well-rationalized, letting you learn from someone else's battle scars.

There are definitely points in here that are debatable, but reading through them will likely give you ideas on how to better design and refine your own REST APIs.
