I enjoyed this fun post about [creating a page to list every UUID that exists](https://eieio.games/blog/writing-down-every-uuid/).

The premise is completely tongue-in-cheek, but it ends up being a fascinating technical challenge to pull off. The author walks through each engineering hurdle—from rendering and scrolling billions of records to compression and browser limitations—and how they ended up solving them. It's an entertaining and clever read.
