date: 2026-04-22T08:15:00-05:00

Anthropic stirred up considerable controversy by testing pricing tiers that restrict Claude Code access exclusively to expensive $100/month or $200/month plans.

[Simon Willison wrote a detailed breakdown of the confusion and his assessment](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/22/claude-code-confusion/). As he noted, even if this was an isolated pricing test, it introduced substantial uncertainty for developers relying on the tool for daily workflows—prompting many to reconsider whether investing time into Claude Code is sustainable versus open or multi-provider alternatives.

Across the industry, AI labs are under increasing pressure to rein in massive compute expenditures and chart a path to profitability. The open question is where developer pricing will ultimately settle, and how the resulting cost-benefit calculus reshapes developer tooling.
