---
title: "Showcase of Google's Nano-Banana Image Model"
date: 2025-10-08T08:00:00-05:00
---

Google released an update to its image generation models called Nano-Banana. While it is highly capable, release notes don't always make it obvious what practical workflows it excels at.

A group [put together a great showcase of sample images featuring various use cases](https://github.com/PicoTrex/Awesome-Nano-Banana-images/blob/main/README_en.md). Some of the examples are really inventive, such as taking a regular photograph of a building, isolating it, and rendering it into a clean isometric view.
