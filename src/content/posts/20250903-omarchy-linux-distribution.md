---
title: "Omarchy: A Keyboard-Centric Linux Distribution"
date: 2025-09-03T08:00:00-05:00
---

David Heinemeier Hansson (DHH) of Ruby on Rails and 37signals fame has created a new Linux distribution called [Omarchy](https://omarchy.org/).

What's compelling about this distro is that it is built entirely around keyboard navigation and tiling window management out of the box. The demo video on the site is really interesting and looks like a polished setup worth trying out for anyone looking for a streamlined, developer-centric desktop.
