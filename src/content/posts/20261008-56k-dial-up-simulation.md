date: 2026-10-08T08:15:00-05:00

If you want a visceral hit of late-90s web nostalgia—or if you weren't around to experience the era of copper-wire modems firsthand—[56k.rip](https://56k.rip/) is an expertly crafted simulation of early dial-up internet browsing.

From the screeching handshake audio to progressive image rendering and random mid-session disconnects, it captures the dial-up experience with painful accuracy.
