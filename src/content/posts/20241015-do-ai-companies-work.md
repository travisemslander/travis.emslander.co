---
title: "Do AI Companies Work?"
date: 2024-10-15T08:00:00-05:00
---

A very interesting hot take argues that [it's hard to see a future for AI companies](https://benn.substack.com/p/do-ai-companies-work). Their costs are out of control, and the author makes the case that this doesn't appear to be a problem that will go away. Any advancement in computing will simply change the goalposts for what they try to achieve using that extra power, never letting these companies reach profit.

It will be fascinating to look back a few years from now on an article like this. Was it right, and will AI companies face a 2000-era dot-com bust? Or was it just missing the next step they would take to become profitable juggernauts?
