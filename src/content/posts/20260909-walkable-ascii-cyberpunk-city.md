Check out this [walkable 3D cyberpunk city rendered entirely in ASCII text](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3YtygAx_C6A).

The developer built a fully navigable 3D world with retro-futuristic aesthetics, lighting effects, and dense city streets, all converted into real-time ASCII characters. You can find and [try the project here](https://ko-fi.com/s/e1e0f91951). It's an incredible technical showcase of creative rendering constraints.
