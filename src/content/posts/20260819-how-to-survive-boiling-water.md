[How to survive boiling water](https://taxa.substack.com/p/how-to-survive-boiling-water) is a fascinating deep dive that has nothing to do with software or AI. Instead, it follows a curious microbiologist down a rabbit hole investigating a seemingly paradoxical claim: "probiotic tea."

While the microbiology details can get deep, the real joy of the essay is following the scientific method in action. That process mirrors software engineering and debugging at its best: formulating hypotheses, isolating variables, conducting disciplined experiments, and determining root cause when the observed reality doesn't match the marketing claims.

It also offers a healthy dose of critical skepticism toward trendy "probiotic" wellness labels that have become ubiquitous on grocery shelves.
