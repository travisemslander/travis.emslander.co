date: 2025-09-17T08:00:00-05:00
---

My wife and I are big fans of Nintendo's *Animal Crossing*. We first got into it years ago on the GameCube and, like many others, revisited it when *New Horizons* came out on the Nintendo Switch. If you aren't familiar, it's a wonderfully eccentric game where you casually stroll around a small village, digging up fossils, decorating your house, and chatting with quirky animal neighbors.

That background made me love reading about [Josh Fonseca integrating an LLM into the 20-year-old GameCube game](https://joshfonseca.com/blogs/animal-crossing-llm). He hooked an LLM up to create dynamic character dialogue on the fly. It was done via emulator rather than original hardware, but the reverse-engineering work and dialogue injection are still remarkably impressive.

He also posted a [YouTube video explaining the project](https://youtu.be/7AyEzA5ziE0?si=_SADxbdz650nyCnP)—though be prepared for some of the most memorable sunglasses you'll ever see.
