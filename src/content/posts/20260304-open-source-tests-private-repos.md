---
title: "Why Open Source Projects Are Moving Test Suites to Private Repos"
date: 2026-03-04T08:00:00-05:00
---

As AI models become increasingly proficient at writing code from detailed specifications, automated tests have become more valuable than ever. In effect, a comprehensive test suite acts as an executable specification of a program's desired behavior.

Because of this, open source projects with commercial business models are realizing their codebases can easily be rewritten and re-licensed by competitors using LLMs guided by their test suites. In response, [projects like tldraw are beginning to move their test cases to private repositories](https://github.com/tldraw/tldraw/issues/8082). It's a fascinating shift in how we think about software engineering assets and open source licensing in an AI world.
