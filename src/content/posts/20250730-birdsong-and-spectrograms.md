I came across a fascinating [YouTube video exploring the acoustics of birdsong](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hCQCP-5g5bo).

The creator uses spectrograms to break down the incredible vocal mechanics birds use to produce complex sounds. One of the most interesting parts is where he synthesizes a sound that encodes an image of a bird into its spectrogram (reminiscent of that [famous Aphex Twin spectrogram Easter egg](https://www.reddit.com/r/Damnthatsinteresting/comments/kvjill/aphex_twins_song_known_as_formula_contains/)), only to discover that certain bird vocalizations naturally form recognizable visual shapes when visualized as sound waves. Very weird, but remarkably cool.
