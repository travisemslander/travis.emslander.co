I came across a [thoughtful comparison of Go, Rust, and Zig](https://sinclairtarget.com/blog/2025/08/thoughts-on-go-vs.-rust-vs.-zig/) by Sinclair Target.

Rather than falling into the usual trap of declaring which language is "best," the author focuses on the underlying philosophies and design priorities of each creator. It provides great clarity on how their differing values—Go's focus on simplicity and concurrency, Rust's emphasis on safety and zero-cost abstractions, and Zig's dedication to simplicity without hidden control flow or runtime overhead—shape how software is written in each.
