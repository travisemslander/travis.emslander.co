title: "Explaining Algorithms with IKEA-Style Instructions"
date: 2025-10-15T08:00:00-05:00
---

IKEA's assembly manuals are famous for being completely wordless: using only diagrams, the same instructions work seamlessly in every country around the globe. [Here's a classic example from the 'BILLY' bookcase](https://www.ikea.com/us/en/assembly_instructions/billy-bookcase-with-glass-doors-dark-blue__AA-1832545-9-100.pdf).

I recently discovered [idea-instructions.com](https://idea-instructions.com/), a site that borrows this exact visual style to explain computer science algorithms and technical concepts. Distilling a complex technical topic down to its bare fundamentals so it can be communicated entirely without text is a fantastic exercise in communication. They even give them faux-Swedish names—[here is their great breakdown of Quicksort, or rather "kvick sört"](https://idea-instructions.com/quick-sort/).
