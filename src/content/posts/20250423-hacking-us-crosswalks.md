There is a very interesting article titled [Hacking US crosswalks to talk like Zuck is as easy as 123](https://www.theregister.com/2025/04/19/us_crosswalk_button_hacking/). It details some clever hackers updating crosswalks that normally have a generic audio message saying "wait... wait..." to play recordings of AI-generated deepfakes of Elon Musk, Mark Zuckerberg, and recently in Seattle, Jeff Bezos. Definitely check out the video in the article for a good laugh.

How did they do this? Turns out there was a publicly available app to admin these on the Android and iOS app stores, and the default password of `1234` often wasn't changed on the crosswalk devices.
