---
title: "HN Update: Audio Summaries of Hacker News"
date: 2024-11-06T08:00:00-06:00
---

I get a lot of interesting articles from Y Combinator's [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/). Someone created a project called [HN Update](https://hnup.date/) that gives you a 15-minute audio update of top stories.

It doesn't sound as polished as Google's NotebookLM podcasts (something to try if you haven't), but what I find cool is that it not only covers the top story, but also talks about some of the comments on Hacker News about the story. There's also a sassy version which is fun. I wish the creator had detailed how they built it—I'd find that technical breakdown very interesting.
