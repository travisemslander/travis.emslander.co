date: 2026-09-09T08:15:00-05:00

Neal Agarwal (of [neal.fun](https://neal.fun/)) released another brilliantly creative web game: [Unusual Suspects](https://neal.fun/unusual-suspects/).

In the game, you play the role of a police sketch artist listening to witness descriptions of a suspect. You draw the composite sketch using the built-in canvas, and an AI model evaluates and scores how closely your drawing matches the actual culprit. It's a clever, hilarious application of vision models and creative web design.
