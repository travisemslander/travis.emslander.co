title: "Livecoding Music with Strudel"
date: 2025-10-22T08:00:00-05:00
---

I happened upon a fascinating [video of Switch Angel making music live by writing code](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GWXCCBsOMSg). At first, I wasn't sure if the code on screen was actively driving the audio synthesizer or just visual flair for the video.

Digging through the comments revealed that she was using [Strudel](https://strudel.cc/), a browser-based livecoding environment for algorithmic music patterns (based on TidalCycles). They have an [interactive tutorial](https://strudel.cc/workshop/getting-started/) that teaches the syntax step by step right in the browser. It's remarkably well executed and a ton of fun to experiment with.
